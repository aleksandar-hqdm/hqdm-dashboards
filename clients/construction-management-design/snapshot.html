12 weeks in · results + the case for 6 more months · CCB #112648

HQDM started on the CM&D account on March 12, 2026. Twelve weeks in, the work that's shipped is starting to show up in the search data. On Google Search Console, the site's impressions are up +112% for the engagement window over the prior 90 days (1,940 → 4,116), clicks are up 32%, and the brand keyword "cm&d" has moved from position 22.1 to position 7.8 — page 3 to top of page 1. Compared to this time last year (April-May 2025), the site went from 0 impressions to 3,650. Ten blog posts have been published in the engagement window, all targeting service-intent informational searches (walk-in shower permits, ADA shower requirements, kitchen rebuilds, insurance rebuilds). The walk-in shower permits post is already at position 7 on Google. On Google Maps, the top-10 grid coverage on your primary brand search is preserved at 99.4% of the 169-cell grid even through the May algorithm pressure, and "construction design" has just started recovering its top-3 footprint (4 → 24 cells in the last 10 days). What hasn't been built yet — the commercial service pages those impressions should route into — is what the next six months are designed to deliver, at the same budget.
