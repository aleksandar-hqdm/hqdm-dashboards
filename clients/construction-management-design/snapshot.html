Construction Management & Design

12 weeks in · results + the case for 6 more months · CCB #112648

HQDM started on the CM&D account on March 12, 2026. Twelve weeks in, two things are now clear in the data: the brand is owned and the content engine works. cmdesigns.net ranks #1 organically for "construction management & design," and of the ten blog posts shipped this quarter, three already rank on page 1 of Google (walk-in shower permits #7, insurance rebuilds #6, rebuild timeline #9). The ADA walk-in-shower guide alone now ranks for a cluster of 44 distinct searches. On Google Search Console, impressions are up +112% over the prior 90 days (1,940 → 4,116), 186 brand-new searches found the site this quarter, and the brand keyword "cm&d" moved from position 22.1 to 7.8. On Maps, top-10 grid coverage on your primary brand search spans the full 169-cell grid, and the most recent four weeks are the strongest yet — your brand searches reached organic #1 and the ADA guide climbed +12.5 positions. The honest gap — and the whole case for the next six months — is that the site ranks for zero commercial service terms in its top 20, because it has never had the service pages those searches require. In your Portland market that work is searched ~3,480 times a month at a $24 blended cost-per-click — roughly $84K/month of demand you can't yet capture. The next six months at the same budget build the pages that capture it.

The real organic footprint — brand owned, commercial layer open

An independent look (DataForSEO) at every keyword cmdesigns.net ranks for tells the clearest version of the story. The site ranks for 44 keywords — and it owns its brand: #1 for "construction management & design," #5 for "cm&d." But all five of its top-20 rankings are brand-name variants. There is not one commercial service term in the top 20. That's not a failure — it's the opportunity stated as a single number: the pages that would rank for "bathroom remodel" or "general contractor" have never existed, so the site has never been eligible. Build them and eligibility follows — into open ground, with no incumbent CM&D page to displace.
