Construction Management & Design

Maps + on-page diagnosis · Portland, OR · CCB #112648

The two Google Maps searches CM&D used to rank on — "construction management" and "construction design" — have been declining sharply through the spring. Between March 5 and May 31 your top-3 visibility on "construction management" fell from 157 grid cells to 2, and "construction design" fell from 103 to 24. Across four other searches that match the work the business actually does — bathroom remodel, walk-in shower conversion, kitchen repair, bathroom remodeling near me — the profile has not appeared in the top 20 on a single one of 169 cells, in any scan, throughout the spring. The root cause is on the website: only one of your eight Google service categories has a matching page on the site (/kitchen-remodeling), there is no schema markup anywhere, and the suburb pages have URL structures that read to Google as automatically generated. The recovery work is all on-page, runs ~90 days, and does not require review, photo or Q&A work.

What's holding CM&D back · 5 findings
Finding 1 · site does not back the GBP
1 of 8 services has a site page
GBP has 27 service items across 6 categories. Site has /kitchen-remodeling. There is no bathroom-remodeling, walk-in-shower-conversion, or kitchen-repair page anywhere on cmdesigns.net.
Finding 2 · suburb URLs signal "auto-generated"
9 spam-pattern slugs
Each suburb URL uses one of 8 junk modifiers (dependable / efficient / trusted / fast / quality / premium / local / skilled) + a generic stem. The underlying copy is actually salvageable — the Marylhurst page is ~950 words with real local references.
Finding 3 · no schema anywhere
0 LocalBusiness · 0 Service · 0 AreaServed
The site emits no structured data for Google to consume. Competitors with 1-2 reviews out-rank CM&D mainly because their site/profile coupling is cleaner.
Finding 4 · no conversion tracking installed
no GA4 · no GTM · no Clarity
The GBP link is correctly tagged (utm_campaign=gbp) but nothing on the site is listening. Lead, call, and visit tracking are blind right now.
Finding 5 · review count below the local pack floor
6 reviews · median 26 · top 841
Context only. Reviews are outside the scope of what HQDM works on for you — flagged here so the team understands where the gap is. The work we propose does not depend on review growth.
Finding 6 · the GBP itself is solid
strong service items · clean description
27+ service items, 6 categories, accurate hours, license listed, 8 suburbs in the description. The profile is not the bottleneck. The site is.
