The story for the renewal call

Lead with two proven assets. (1) The brand is owned — cmdesigns.net ranks #1 organically for "construction management & design" and #5 for "cm&d," the authority most contractors never secure. (2) The content engine works — of 10 blog posts shipped, three already rank on page 1 (permits #7, insurance rebuilds #6, rebuild timeline #9), the ADA guide pulls a cluster of 44 distinct queries, 186 brand-new queries found the site this quarter, and impressions are +112%. Maps top-10 on the primary brand query held at 99.4% through May algo pressure; construction-design top-3 just bounced 4→24 in 10 days. The honest gap and the entire renewal case: CM&D ranks for zero commercial service terms in its top 20 because the site has never had the service pages those terms require. The Portland metro searches for this work ~3,480×/month at a $24 blended CPC ≈ $84K/month in ad-equivalent value ("bathroom remodel Portland OR" alone is a $51 CPC). The next 6 months convert proven content capability + owned brand authority into booked jobs — direction continuity, not new investment.

What's left to address · 4 gaps

  1. P0 Site backs only 1 of 8 GBP services. The blog cohort built footprint; the service pages convert it. Build /bathroom-remodeling/ + /walk-in-shower-conversion/ + /kitchen-repair/ + /general-contractor/ in M4-M6.
  2. P0 Spam-pattern suburb URLs. 9 suburb pages with junk-modifier prefixes. Migrate to /west-linn/, /marylhurst/, etc. with 301s in M4. Underlying copy keeps.
  3. P0 Zero schema everywhere. JSON-LD via Wix custom code in M4 — single rollout. Single biggest technical lift on Maps.
  4. P1 No tracking installed. GTM + GA4 + Clarity + 4-event model in M4. First clean attribution month in August (M6).

Reviews stay out of scope. Context for the renewal call: CM&D has 6 reviews · leaderboard median 26 · NW Tub & Shower leads at 841. The name-match competitors winning ahead of CM&D have 1-2 reviews themselves, so reviews are a long-term ceiling but not the binding constraint right now.

D3 · P1 · HQDM on-page + dev
